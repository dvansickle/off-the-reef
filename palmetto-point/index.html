Palmetto Point and around

North Palmetto Point is a quiet stretch of central Eleuthera, a few minutes south of Governor's Harbour. Most of what you'll want is within a short drive, and the rest of the island is a day trip.

Bank Road

Bank Road runs along the Atlantic side of North Palmetto Point, with houses set back behind sea grape on one side and the beach on the other. It's a residential road with little traffic, good for an early walk or bike ride.

Governor's Harbour

The island's historic center, on the Caribbean side, with a harbor, old wooden houses up the hill, a library, a few shops and the airport just north of town. It's the closest place for errands, banking and a meal out.

Your picks: bakery, coffee, the place you go on Friday night.

Shops on Eleuthera keep shorter hours than at home and many close on Sundays. Stock up on arrival day.

Day trips

  • North: Glass Window Bridge, where the island narrows to a strip of rock between the deep blue Atlantic and the green Bight. Harbour Island is a short water-taxi ride from North Eleuthera.
  • South: Lighthouse Beach and the cliffs at Bannerman Town, at the far southern tip. Allow a full day and bring lunch and water.
  • Either side: the island is narrow, so a Caribbean-side beach is never far when the Atlantic is rough.
