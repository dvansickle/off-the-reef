Getting here

Fly into Governor's Harbour, pick up a car, and you're at the house in minutes.

By air

Governor's Harbour Airport (GHB) is the closest airport to North Palmetto Point. Flights reach it from Nassau and from South Florida; airlines and schedules change by season, so check current options when you plan.

Eleuthera also has airports at North Eleuthera (ELH) and Rock Sound (RSD). Both are a longer drive from the house, so use them only if the timing works much better.

By ferry

Bahamas Ferries runs service between Nassau and Eleuthera, including Governor's Harbour on some sailings. It's a good option if you're already in Nassau or traveling with a lot of luggage. Check the current schedule before you plan around it.

Getting around

You'll want a car. The island is about 110 miles long, there's no public transit to speak of, and taxis add up quickly. Driving is on the left.
