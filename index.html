The house

A small cottage set back from the beach behind sea grape and privacy trees, on a lot that runs from Bank Road down to the water. Mornings on the screened porch, evenings on the front patio under the timber pergola, and the beach a short walk through the garden.

The beach and the reef

The house sits on Eleuthera's Atlantic side, where a reef runs offshore and breaks the ocean swell before it reaches the beach. Inside the reef the water is usually calm and clear enough for swimming and snorkeling straight from the sand. On rough days you can watch the surf line out on the reef from the porch.
